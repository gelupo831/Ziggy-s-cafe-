A little context

Ziggy’s Cafe is a long-running breakfast and lunch diner in Westbrooke Shopping Center on U.S. Highway 70 in Havelock, near Marine Corps Air Station Cherry Point. The room is small and often busy with regulars, especially on weekend mornings. There is a covered walkway out front, and the printed table-tent asks guests to call ahead for carry-out.

In a December 20, 2025 WITN story about the new U.S. 70 bypass, cafe employee Rebecca Kronenberg said the staff had heard of other bypassed towns drying up, but they were staying optimistic that business would continue.

This page is an unofficial dossier drafted from public listings and photos. It is not the cafe’s website, and the cafe has not reviewed it.
